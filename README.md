# 🏥 Healthcare Stay & Billing Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates the application of **Business Intelligence (BI) in the healthcare sector** using Microsoft Power BI.

Developed collaboratively with college teammates as part of a university assignment, this project transforms healthcare data into meaningful insights through an interactive dashboard.

The dashboard focuses on patient admissions, hospital billing, medical conditions, admission types, and length of stay to support data-driven analysis and healthcare operational planning.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze patient admissions and hospital billing.
- Calculate key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Explore medical conditions and admission types.
- Compare average billing across medical conditions and hospitals.
- Analyze average patient age and length of stay.
- Identify patterns and trends in healthcare data.
- Support data-driven decision-making through interactive visualizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard creation and data visualization.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** — KPI calculations and analytical measures.
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation.
- **Kaggle** — Healthcare dataset source.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Admissions | Total number of patient admission records. |
| Total Billing | Total billing amount across the included records. |
| Average Billing | Average billing amount per record. |
| Average Length of Stay | Average duration of patient hospitalization. |
| Average Age | Average age of admitted patients. |
| Admissions Percentage | Percentage of admissions associated with each medical condition. |

## 🔍 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Medical Conditions Analysis
Analyzes the distribution of patient admissions across different medical conditions to identify the conditions with the largest share of admissions.

### 2. Billing Analysis
Compares average billing across medical conditions to identify categories associated with higher average billing in the dataset.

### 3. Length of Stay Analysis
Examines the average duration of hospitalization for different medical conditions to understand differences in patient stay patterns.

### 4. Admission Type Analysis
Compares average billing across admission types, including elective admissions.

### 5. Hospital Analysis
Compares hospitals based on admission volume and average billing to explore differences in hospital activity and billing patterns.

### 6. Monthly Billing Analysis
Examines monthly billing patterns to identify months with higher average billing.

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard visualizations:

- **Arthritis** has the highest share of admissions among the medical conditions displayed.
- **Asthma** has the highest average length of stay among the medical conditions shown.
- **Diabetes** has the highest average billing among the medical conditions displayed.
- **Elective admissions** have the highest average billing among the admission types shown.
- Hospital comparisons reveal differences in admission volume and average billing.
- **June and October** recorded the highest average billing in the monthly analysis, at approximately **2.59M**, according to the dashboard results.

*Note: These insights reflect the current dashboard results and should be validated against the underlying dataset and DAX calculations.*
